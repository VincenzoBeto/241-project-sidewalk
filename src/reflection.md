5. What was the most challenging aspect of this assignment? Why?
     The most challenging aspect of this assignment was optimizing the program and removing unnecessary steps. I had started with two HashMaps in the compute function, where one was used for the computation and the other was for the final result, but that wasn't needed as copying them was redundant. I also started by adding all nodes to the path map, then updating them when found, but thanks to the precondition that all nodes are in the graph for accessing methods this could be skipped, making the code easier to read and faster.
